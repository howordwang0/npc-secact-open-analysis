# STROBE Statement checklist

**Manuscript:** Patient-level multimodal localization and robustness testing of
treatment-associated secreted-protein activity in nasopharyngeal carcinoma

**Article design:** Retrospective secondary analysis of multiple open
observational transcriptomic cohorts. The cohort checklist is applied to the
patient-level clinical components; cellular, region, and spatial analyses use
patients/specimens as inferential units.
